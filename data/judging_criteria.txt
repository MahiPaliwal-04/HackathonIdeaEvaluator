HACKATHON JUDGING CRITERIA

Problem Relevance:
The idea should solve a real and meaningful problem. Judges evaluate whether the problem affects a significant group of users and whether the proposed solution addresses the problem clearly.

Innovation:
The idea should have a creative or novel approach. Using existing technology in a meaningful new way can also be considered innovative.

Technical Feasibility:
The proposed solution should be technically possible to implement with available technologies, resources, time and skills.

Impact:
The solution should provide measurable benefits to users, society, businesses, education, healthcare or the environment.

User Experience:
The solution should be easy to understand and useful for the intended users.

Scalability:
The solution should have potential to expand to more users, locations or use cases.

Sustainability:
The idea should be maintainable and have a realistic long-term implementation plan.

Judging usually considers:
Problem Relevance
Innovation
Technical Feasibility
Impact
User Experience
Scalability
Sustainability
